# Future ideas

These are exploratory ideas, not committed gameplay requirements.

## Obstacles

Add physical barriers that Splurgs cannot pass through. Before implementation, decide how pathfinding should behave when a creature is trapped or a route is blocked, and ensure the renderer and world-boundary rules agree on obstacle placement.

## Fauna

Add non-Splurg food sources that creatures can consume. Model the energy introduced and removed by fauna first so the new resource does not unintentionally destabilize the simulation's energy balance. Decide whether fauna replenishes, moves, or can be depleted.
